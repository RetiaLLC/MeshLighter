MeshLighter

A browser-based Meshtastic research suite. Visualize the public mesh in real time, script the radio over Web Serial, and — with authorization — exercise the injection tooling. Read-only tools are open to everyone; anything that transmits asks for an authorization affirmation first.

Authorized use only.

The transmit-capable tools here (firmware install, packet injection/spoofing) are for security research and education on hardware and RF you own or are explicitly authorized to test. Transmitting on the 900 MHz band or injecting into a Meshtastic network you do not own may be illegal. Those actions are gated behind an affirmation; the visualizers and passive scripting never transmit.
